# NYC Zoning Resolution - Article IV (Manufacturing District Regulations)
# DEMO CORPUS: plain-language paraphrase for development. Replace with official text from zr.planning.nyc.gov.

There are three basic manufacturing districts: M1, M2, and M3.

M1 districts are light manufacturing districts, often serving as buffers between M2 or M3 districts and adjacent residential or commercial districts.
M1 districts typically include light industrial uses such as woodworking shops, repair shops, and wholesale service and storage facilities, and many commercial uses are also permitted.

M2 districts occupy the middle ground between light and heavy industrial areas.

M3 districts are designated for heavy industrial uses that generate noise, traffic, or pollutants, such as power plants, solid waste transfer facilities, and fuel supply depots.

Manufacturing uses must meet performance standards that limit noise, vibration, smoke, odor, and other emissions.
Performance standards are generally stricter in M1 districts than in M3 districts.

New residential uses are generally not permitted in manufacturing districts.
Mixed Use (MX) districts pair an M1 district with a residence district, such as M1-2/R6, so that residential and light industrial uses can be located side by side or within the same building.
